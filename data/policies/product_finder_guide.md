# SteelShop Product Finder Guide

## How To Ask In Chat
- “Tell me about Wireless Earbuds Pro” → full product sheet
- “Compare earbuds” or “Compare SKU-EAR-PRO and SKU-EAR-LITE” → side-by-side specs
- “What keyboard is best for a small desk?” → catalog search + policy tips
- “Specs for Ergonomic Chair” → warranty months, capacity, armrests

## Catalog Categories
Audio (earbuds), peripherals (keyboards), desk (monitor arms), accessories (hubs/docks), furniture (chairs).

## Compare Groups
Products share a compare_group so the bot can pair peers: earbuds, keyboard, monitor_arm, hub, chair.

## Data Sources
Structured catalog JSON powers exact lookup/compare. The same catalog is ingested into the knowledge base so policy RAG can cite product facts.
