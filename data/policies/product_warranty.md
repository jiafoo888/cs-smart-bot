# SteelShop Product Warranty

## Standard Warranty
Most electronics include a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects under normal use. Accessories such as cables may carry a 6-month warranty.

## What Is Covered
Defects in materials or workmanship. Warranty service may include repair, replacement, or store credit at SteelShop's discretion.

## What Is Not Covered
Damage from misuse, liquid exposure, unauthorized repair, normal wear, or missing proof of purchase. Software issues caused by third-party apps are excluded.

## How To Claim
Provide the order id (ORD-xxxx), product serial if available, and photos of the defect. Claims within the return window may be faster via the return flow.

## Extended Care
Optional extended care plans add 12 extra months and accidental damage coverage (one claim per year, deductible may apply).
