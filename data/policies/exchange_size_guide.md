# SteelShop Exchange & Size Guide

## Exchange Window
Exchanges for size/color are accepted within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused with original packaging. Electronics must include all accessories.

## How Exchange Works
1. Start an exchange request in chat with your order id.
2. We approve eligible items and email a prepaid label when required.
3. After warehouse QC, we ship the replacement (same SKU different option, or peer SKU if stock requires).

## Not Eligible
Activated software, clearance final-sale, customized engraving, and opened consumables.

## Size Tips
- Earbuds: try all tip sizes; poor seal often feels like weak bass, not a defect.
- Chairs: Ergonomic Chair Plus has deeper seat and higher weight rating for taller users.
- Monitor arms: confirm VESA pattern and panel weight before ordering Dual vs Single.
