# SteelShop Privacy & Account Security Policy

## Personal Data Use
We use necessary personal data only for order fulfillment, customer support, and compliance.
We do not sell personal data.

## Account Security
Use the "Forgot password" flow to recover access. Agents cannot reveal or set plaintext passwords.
If an account may be compromised, change the password immediately and escalate to freeze risky orders.

## Conversation Audit
Bot conversations may be stored in audit logs for quality review, dispute evidence, and model improvement,
subject to internal retention rules.

## Third-Party Sharing
Tracking numbers are shared with carriers. Payment details are processed by payment providers;
SteelShop does not store full card numbers.
