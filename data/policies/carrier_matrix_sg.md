# Last-mile carrier matrix (Singapore)

## Ninja Van
Best for: B2C parcels under 5 kg island-wide.
Typical transit: **1–2 business days** after SteelHub handover.
Coverage: all mainland Singapore postal districts; selected Sentosa lanes.

## SingPost
Best for: document packs, light parcels, PO Box destinations.
Typical transit: **2–4 business days**.
Use when recipient prefers letterbox / Post Office collect.

## GrabExpress
Best for: urgent same-day, fragile, or high-value under 20 kg within mainland Singapore.
Booked only when shipper selects **express** and release happens before **15:00 SGT**.
Higher surcharge applies; ETA often **2–6 hours** after pickup from DC.

## Compare summary
| Need | Suggested carrier |
|------|-------------------|
| Cost-efficient B2C | Ninja Van |
| Documents / PO Box | SingPost |
| Same-day urgent | GrabExpress |

SteelHub does not guarantee carrier SLA beyond the contracted handover; last-mile exceptions follow the carrier's public SLA plus our claims window.
