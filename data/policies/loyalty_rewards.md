# SteelShop Loyalty & Rewards

## Points Basics
Earn 1 point per ¥1 spent on eligible orders after delivery. Points appear within 48 hours of delivery confirmation.

## Redemption
100 points = ¥1 store credit at checkout. Minimum redemption is 500 points. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Tier Benefits
- Standard: base earning rate
- Gold (≥1000 points lifetime): free shipping vouchers each quarter
- VIP (≥2000 points or invite): priority support SLA and +10% points on electronics

## Coupons
Account coupons stack with points unless marked exclusive. Expired or used codes cannot be restored by chat agents.

## Expiry
Points expire 24 months after the earn date. We email a reminder 30 days before expiry.
