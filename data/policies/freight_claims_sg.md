# Damaged / lost freight claims (Singapore)

## Claim window
Shippers must open a damage or short-shipment claim within **7 calendar days** of delivery scan (or expected delivery date if lost).
Claims after 7 days are reviewed case-by-case and may be declined.

## Evidence required
- Shipment id (`SHP-xxxx`) or AWB
- Photos of outer carton + inner goods damage
- Packing list / ASN reference
- Brief description of loss or damage

## Liability cap (demo policy)
Unless otherwise contracted, SteelHub warehouse liability for handling damage is capped at **SGD 200** per shipment or declared value, whichever is lower.
Carrier last-mile liability follows the selected carrier's terms.

## Process
1. CS opens ticket with priority `high` (SLA 60 minutes first response).
2. Ops confirms receiving photos / outbound photos from WMS.
3. Credit note or replacement ASN is offered within **3 business days** when claim is approved.
