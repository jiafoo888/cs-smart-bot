# GST and invoice notes (Singapore) — high level

## Important
This is **operational guidance for the demo**, not tax advice. Shippers should consult their accountant for GST filing.

## Tax invoice
SteelHub issues tax invoices in **SGD** for storage, fulfilment, and last-mile pass-through fees when the shipper account is GST-registered and billed in Singapore.
Invoice numbers look like `INV-xxxx`.

## GST rate
Demo copy assumes the prevailing Singapore GST rate shown on invoices (e.g. **9%**). Line items show net + GST where applicable.

## E-invoice request
Ask the bot for an invoice with your `SHP-xxxx` (fulfilment charge) or account id. Corporate invoices need UEN / tax id on file.

## Cross-border
Import GST / duty for inbound overseas freight is the shipper's responsibility unless a DDP arrangement is contracted separately.
