# Inbound receiving SLA (Singapore)

## Cut-off and dock booking
SteelHub Jurong West DC (postal `640903`) accepts inbound ASN bookings **Mon–Sat 08:00–18:00 SGT**.
ASN must be submitted at least **4 business hours** before the booked dock slot.

## Receiving windows
- Standard pallet inbound: dock-to-putaway within **4 hours** after truck arrival (SGT business hours).
- Express / temperature-controlled: dock-to-putaway within **2 hours**.
- Same-day ASN without prior booking may be refused or rolled to next available slot.

## ASN requirements
Each ASN must include: shipper account, SKU list, expected qty, pallet count, and vehicle plate when known.
Missing ASN → goods held in quarantine zone until paperwork is complete (up to **1 business day**).

## Exceptions
Damaged cartons found at receiving are photographed and reported within **2 hours**. Shipper claims window starts from report time (see freight claims policy).
