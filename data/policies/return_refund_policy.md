# SteelShop Return & Refund Policy

## No-Reason Return Window
Within 7 days of delivery, unused items with original packaging and accessories may be returned
for any reason. Clearance, customized, and activated software/account products are excluded.

## Quality-Issue Returns
After 7 days, returns are accepted only for verified quality issues with photo/video evidence.
If approved, SteelShop covers return shipping.

## Refund Timeline
After approval, refunds go back to the original payment method:
- WeChat Pay / Alipay: usually 1–3 business days
- Bank card: usually 3–7 business days
When complete, order status becomes `refunded` and the related payment status becomes `refunded`.

## Partial Refunds
Partial refunds apply only when an order contains multiple items and only some are returned.
Single-item orders default to a full refund.

## Cancel vs Refund
Orders in `processing` or `paid` (not yet shipped) should be cancelled, not refunded.
Shipped/delivered orders must use the return-and-refund flow.
