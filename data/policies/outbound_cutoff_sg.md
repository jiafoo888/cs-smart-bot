# Outbound cut-off (Singapore DC)

## Same-day dispatch
Orders released to WMS before **14:00 SGT** on business days are eligible for same-day carrier handover from Jurong West DC.
After 14:00 SGT → next business day dispatch unless marked **priority** (gold/VIP shipper).

## Peak periods
During **11.11**, **12.12**, and **CNY week**, same-day cut-off moves to **12:00 SGT**. Extra transit buffer of +1 day applies to last-mile ETAs.

## Carrier handover
SteelHub hands parcels to contracted last-mile partners at the DC yard. Tracking updates typically appear within **60–90 minutes** after handover.

## Tuas cross-dock
Cross-border / Malaysia lane freight uses Tuas staging. Cut-off for Tuas transfer trucks is **16:00 SGT**.
