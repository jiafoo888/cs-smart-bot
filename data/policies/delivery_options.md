# SteelShop Delivery Options

## Standard Shipping
3–5 business days after warehouse handoff for major cities. Tracking updates usually appear within 24 hours of pickup.

## Express
1–2 business days where available. Express fees show at checkout; VIP members get one free express upgrade per quarter on electronics under ¥500.

## White-glove Furniture
Ergonomic chairs and large furniture include appointment delivery and basic assembly positioning. Reschedule at least 24 hours ahead.

## Delays
Weather, peak sales, and remote regions may add 1–3 days. Use chat “nudge fulfillment” only for paid/processing orders still in warehouse.
